- X posts are text only (no media), so none need media approval.

- Reel rotation: Mon EN, Tue HP, Wed HA, Thu SF, Fri EN, then Mon HP, Tue HA, Wed SF, Thu EN, Fri HP. Week 3 picks up at HA.
