## Contradiction Inventory

The committed design already separated operator issuance from ordinary request
use and named the store as durable claim owner. Four remaining evidence seams
needed explicit normalization:

1. the short durable-state sentence omitted `ABORTED` from its list;
2. “atomic” transition/consume language did not define a consume persistence
   failure outcome;
3. observability omitted `ACTIVATION_AUTH_ABORTED`; and
4. one proposed test-file key was named for EA-4E.59 despite targeting EA-4E.60.

The normalized design evidence now includes an authoritative post-commit block
that supersedes any conflicting interpretation.

## Consume Persistence Failure

If activation is accepted but durable consume cannot be confirmed, the request
must not proceed to invocation authorization or runtime. Request-scoped
activation is torn down immediately. The request remains recovery-blocked until
the store durably records `ABORTED`; there is no retry or reissuance.

```ini
ACTIVATION_ACCEPTED_BUT_CONSUME_PERSISTENCE_FAILS_POLICY=deny continuation; tear activation down to DISABLED; preserve recovery-blocked uncertainty until durable ABORTED confirmation
```
