```text
EXECUTOR_REGISTRY_REGISTER_SEMANTICS=writes executor object into _executors[receiver_id]
```

`bound_count` is a misnomer for registration occupancy. It does not count `ProductionExecutorBindingHandle`s.

`test_replay_collision_registry_not_mutated` evaluates policy with `current_binding_count=1` while `executor_registry.bound_count == 0`. Historical tests treat the policy count as independent of registration occupancy.

## Historical Intent

```text
HISTORICAL_TESTS_DEFINE_REGISTRATION_AS_BINDING=NO
HISTORICAL_TESTS_DEFINE_ACTIVE_HANDLE_AS_BINDING=YES
HISTORICAL_EVIDENCE_SUPPORTS_SEMANTIC_SEPARATION=YES
```

Historical bind tests start with an empty registry and pass `executor_factory`. After bind they assert `registry.bound_count == 1` because bind itself registers. They never pre-register then bind. EA-4E.21 handoff classifies `BINDING_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` as a genuinely new second binding, not as registration occupancy.
