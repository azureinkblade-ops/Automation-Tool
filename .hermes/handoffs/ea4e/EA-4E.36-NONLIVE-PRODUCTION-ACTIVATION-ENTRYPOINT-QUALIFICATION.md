## Activation Path Map

EA-4E.35 `ProductionComposition.invoke` remains config-gated (`master_enable` plus `production_activation_default`). It is not the per-request application facade.

EA-4E.36 adds `ProductionEntryPoint.handle` as the production-facing activation entry point.

```text
PRODUCTION_ENTRYPOINT=tools.hermes_core.production_entrypoint.ProductionEntryPoint.handle
MASTER_ENABLE_CHECK_SITE=ProductionEntryPoint.handle (first gate)
PRODUCTION_ACTIVATION_CHECK_SITE=ProductionEntryPoint.handle (request.production_activation_explicit)
EXPLICIT_RECEIVER_VALIDATION_SITE=classify_receiver
ROUTING_HANDOFF_SITE=ReceiverRouter.route
AUTHORITY_VALIDATION_SITE=GovernedProductionRuntime -> ProductionIssuancePolicy / ExecutionAuthorityValidator
BINDING_HANDOFF_SITE=ProductionExecutorBindingController.get_binding_for_receiver (explicit prior bind required)
INVOCATION_AUTHORIZATION_HANDOFF_SITE=GovernedProductionCaller.invoke -> ProductionInvocationAuthorizationIssuer
RUNTIME_HANDOFF_SITE=GovernedProductionRuntime.execute
PRODUCTION_ACTIVATION_PATH_MAPPED=YES
```

`app.py` remains disconnected.
