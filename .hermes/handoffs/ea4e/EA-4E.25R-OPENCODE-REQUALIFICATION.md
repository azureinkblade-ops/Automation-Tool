## Lessons

1. **Identity STOP condition is mandatory**: EA-4E.25 should have STOPped when the real executor identity did not match the canonical identity. The remediation (EA-4E.25A) verified the narrow production-code fix was valid because the frozen contract identity was correct and only the real executor property was wrong.

2. **Requalification after remediation is valid**: EA-4E.25R proves the corrected implementation works end-to-end through the complete governance chain with no identity masking, actual-event accounting, and exact expected output.

3. **Evidence must be preserved accurately**: The historical defect (identity stop condition violation) is preserved in the evidence, not erased. The qualification status reflects the full sequence: original HOLD → remediation → requalification PASS.
