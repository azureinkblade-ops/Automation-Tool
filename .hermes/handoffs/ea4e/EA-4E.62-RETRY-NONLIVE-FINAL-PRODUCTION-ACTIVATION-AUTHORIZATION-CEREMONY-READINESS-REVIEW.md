### Store Identity And Epoch

The v2 SQLite store persists a UUID store identity, integer epoch, and
installation identity. An external hash anchor binds those values and the
absolute store path. Normal restart preserves identity and epoch. Explicit
rotation increments epoch and terminalizes outstanding authority. Explicit
restore preserves the destination domain, increments destination epoch, and
terminalizes restored outstanding authority. Clone, replacement, rollback, and
anchor mismatch fail closed.
